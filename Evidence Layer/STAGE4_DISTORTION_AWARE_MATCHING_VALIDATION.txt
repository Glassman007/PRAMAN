DISTORTION SEMANTICS
--------------------
Observation-level distortion context is derived only from exact recorded SOURCE_OBSERVATIONS.notes signals:
- slight systematic cadastral shift
- local spatial distortion
- old cadastral geometry contradicted by GNSS

Parcel-level distortion context is derived only from exact recorded RECONCILED_PARCELS.criticality_reason signals:
- Slight spatial shift but identity remains strong.
- Local scale/rotation distortion affects boundaries.
- High-accuracy survey materially contradicts old cadastral geometry.

No geometry correction is simulated in Stage 4.
